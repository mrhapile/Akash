# Review

Check the work before it becomes a problem.

## Use when

- Validate that the implementation matched the plan and fits the architecture.

## Do

- Compare the finished work against the original plan.
- Call out regressions, missing edges, and mismatched assumptions.
- Keep the feedback specific and actionable.

## Avoid

- Treating a quick skim as a full review.
- Auto-fixing instead of reporting issues clearly.
